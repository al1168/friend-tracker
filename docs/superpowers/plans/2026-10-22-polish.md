# Polish Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ] `) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make the numbers fair, make history read as people, keep every word whole at the largest text sizes, and put a friend's memories before their numbers: Stats compares this year with last year up to the same date and never with a year before the first meetup; meetup rows are titled by who was there and the search finds the weather; rows wrap between words, a row's photo moves under its text at accessibility sizes, the back label isn't cut short and Who tiles widen; a friend's History comes before the numbers with "Show all", and a friend with no meetups gets one calm line; plus eight small fixes.

**Architecture:** `src/stats/counts.ts` gains `firstYear`, `sameDateLastYear`, `totalsByDateLastYear` and `countsByFriendByDateLastYear` (pure), with `formatMonthDay` in `src/lib/dates.ts`; Stats and the friend page leave out a comparison year before `firstYear`, and `HBarList` and `MonthBars` take the year before as optional. `friendsNotSeenInYear` reads `createdAt`. `MeetupRow` builds its title with `meetupTitle` from short names (`shortNameById`) and its subtitle with `meetupSubtitle`, which starts with the type; `meetupSearchText` adds the weather's labels. Every inset group body is an inline-size container, so a meetup row with a photo becomes a two-row grid at accessibility sizes; `.navbar__title` takes no width while the large title shows; the Who grid and strip have a 4.25rem minimum column. `FriendDetailScreen` orders History before the stat row, shows `FRIEND_HISTORY_ROWS` (5) from `monthSections` with `firstRows`, and keeps "Show all" in the place memory; `StatRow` items gain a `note`. `Chip` gains `truncate`; "Logged with" uses `shortNames`; the toast reads `--toast-bg` and `--toast-ink`; a `body:has(.page … :focus)` rule hides the Log pill while a page field is typed in. No schema change.

**Tech Stack:** Vite 8, React 19, TypeScript 6 (strict, `verbatimModuleSyntax`, `erasableSyntaxOnly`, `noUnusedLocals`, `noUnusedParameters`), react-router 7 (HashRouter), Dexie 4 with dexie-react-hooks, Vitest 5 with fake-indexeddb (TZ America/Los_Angeles), Playwright WebKit (iPhone 13, port 4174, service workers blocked).

## Decisions made while building

The spec records these at its end ("Decisions made while building"), in Task 6 (and the last one with this plan).

1. **`firstYear`** covers every meetup (upcoming ones can't be earlier than this year); the friend page compares with last year by the app's first year, not the friend's. The year switcher keeps a 44px height with the year alone.
2. **The same date** is worked out on the ISO text ("02-29" becomes "02-28"); `totalsOf` and `countByFriend` are shared, so a friend listed twice counts once.
3. **"Not seen in"** reads `createdAt` on the phone's calendar (`getFullYear()`); one that isn't a date counts as added.
4. **Short names:** `shortNameById` gives what `shortName(friend, friends)` gives, in one pass; the Meetups page keeps full names for the search.
5. **`friendLink`** (e2e) matches `a[href^="#/friends/"]` too, since a meetup row's name can start with a friend's name; tests that opened a row by `/^Coffee/` match `/Coffee/`.
6. **"Show all"** lives in `FriendHistory`, keyed by the history entry, and records `count: Infinity` in the place memory; focus goes to the 6th row's link with `preventScroll`.
7. **Numbers on the friend page** show only once a meetup has happened; "Last year"'s note only when last year had one.
8. **The photo under the text** keys on `.row:has(> .row__trailing > .meetup-thumb)` inside `@container inset-group (width < 14.5em)`.
9. **Who's still grid** is `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(max(4.25rem, 22%), 1fr))`: four across at the default size with room against rounding.
10. **The Log pill steps aside by CSS** (`body:has(.page … :focus) > .log-button`), not focus listeners: a focused field removed from the page needn't fire `focusout`.
11. **The large text toast tests** log with two Bartholomews, so the toast still wraps on full names.
12. **Screenshots:** `31-friend-no-meetups`, `ax-03-log-sheet`, `ax-19-meetups`, `ax-04-friend`, `ax-05-long-name`; still 15 screenshot tests.
13. **The long screenshot captures get 180 seconds** (Task 7): with the bigger seed, "large text" passed in the clone but ran past 120 seconds in the real repo.
