# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person: the owner, on their iPhone, usually one-handed. Two moments matter:

- **Logging**, right after a meetup: walking home from dinner, in the back of a car, at night. They want it done in a few taps and under ten seconds.
- **Reflecting**, occasionally: a slow Sunday morning, scrolling to see who they haven't seen in a while and how this year compares with last year.

## Product Purpose

A private memory aid for friendships. It records when you met which friends, and it answers "when did I last see them?" and "how much have we seen each other this year vs last year?" at a glance.

Success: logging a meetup takes under ten seconds, and opening the app tells you when you last saw anyone without digging.

## Brand Personality

Calm, cute, minimal. It feels like one of Apple's own apps with a little extra warmth: soft and friendly, never loud. The voice is kind and plain, like a note to yourself.

## Anti-references

- **A business CRM.** Friends are not leads. No pipelines, dense tables, or cold "last contacted" language.
- **Guilt-trip streaks.** No Duolingo-style pressure, red alerts, or "you're losing touch!" shaming.
- **A social media feed.** Not busy, noisy, attention-grabbing, or endlessly scrolling.
- **A generic template app.** No purple gradients, identical card grids, or anything that looks AI-generated.

## Design Principles

1. **Logging is effortless.** The most frequent action is always one thumb-reach away, with smart defaults (today, recent friends) so most logs are a few taps.
2. **Facts, not judgment.** Show dates and counts plainly. Never color, word, or rank things to make the user feel bad.
3. **People before numbers.** Faces and names lead; statistics support them.
4. **Feels native to iOS.** Familiar iPhone patterns (tab bar, sheets, inset lists, large titles) over invented ones.
5. **Private by design.** Data stays on the phone, and the interface never implies otherwise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both light and dark mode.
- Respects the iPhone text-size setting (Dynamic Type) without breaking layouts.
- Respects Reduce Motion: animations fall back to simple fades.
- Color is never the only signal (e.g. chart series are also labeled).
- Every control has a VoiceOver label; tap targets are at least 44×44pt.
